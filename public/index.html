Connect your personal Google account once to allow saving PDFs & Photos directly into your personal Google Drive without any Service Account quota limits.

Connect Personal Google Drive Account
GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID="your-oauth-client-id.apps.googleusercontent.com"
GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET="GOCSPX-your-client-secret"
GOOGLE_REFRESH_TOKEN="1//04your-generated-refresh-token"
GOOGLE_DRIVE_FOLDER_ID="1A2b3C4d5E6f7G8h9I0j"

Personal Gmail 3-Step Setup Guide:

  1. In Google Cloud Console, create an OAuth 2.0 Web Client ID and set Redirect URI to https://your-site.vercel.app/api/auth/google.
  2. Add GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID and GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET to Vercel Environment Variables.
  3. Click the Connect Personal Google Drive Account button above to get your GOOGLE_REFRESH_TOKEN!
